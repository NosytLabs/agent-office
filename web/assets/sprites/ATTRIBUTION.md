# Sprite Assets

Character, pet, and furniture sprites adapted from [pixel-agents](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents) by Pablo De Lucca — MIT License. Character sheets: 112×96 PNG, 3 direction rows (down/up/right) × 7 frames of 16×32. Pet sheets: 96×96 (walk/idle rows, left = horizontal flip).
